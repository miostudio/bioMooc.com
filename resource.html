氨基酸缩写(n=20)

中文名 Name 3-Letter code 1-Letter code Side chain Desc
甘氨酸 Glycine Gly G H Optically inactive.
丙氨酸AlanineAla A -CH3 The simplest optically active AA. All other AAs will build off alanine, A like the beginning of the alphabet.
缬氨酸ValineVal V -CH(CH3)2 The side chain is branched like a V.
亮氨酸Leucine Leu L -CH2[CH(CH3)2] The side chain is branched like a Y.
异亮氨酸Isoleucine Ile I -CH(CH3)(C2H5) An isomer of leucine. One branch is longer than the other.
脯氨酸Proline Pro P -CH2 CH2CH2– The only cyclic aliphatic.
半胱氨酸Cysteine Cys C -CH2-SH Cysteine has a sulfhydryl (SH) group.
甲硫氨酸/蛋氨酸Methionine Met M -(CH2)-S-(CH3) Thiol is synonymous with sulfur. Methionine is a methylated sulfur.
苯丙氨酸Phenylalanine Phe F -CH2(C6H5) A benzene (or phenyl) ring attached to an alanine. F like its name.
色氨酸Tryptophan Trp W -indole ring AA with the largest side chain, two fused rings (double).
酪氨酸Tyrosine Tyr Y -CH2(C6H4OH) A hydroxylated (OH) phenylalanine.
丝氨酸Serine Ser S -CH2OH A hydroxylated alanine.
苏氨酸Threonine Thr T – CH(OH)(CH3) Three parts to this AA: alanine, methyl, and hydroxyl.
精氨酸Arginine Arg R -(CH2)3-urea The side chain has a urea (NH2-C (NH2+)(NH)) group (A pirates favorite AA, arginine).
赖氨酸Lysine Lys K -(CH2)4-NH3+ The basic straight chain AA with amino group .
组氨酸Histidine His H -CH2-(C3N2H4)+ This amino acid has an imidazole, an aromatic ring with two nitrogen atoms that can be protonated.
天冬氨酸Aspartate Asp D -CH2COOH An acidic alanine. Just remember D.
谷氨酸Glutamate Glu E -CH2CH2COOH A longer version of Asp. E follows D in the alphabet.
天冬酰胺Asparagine Asn N -CH2CONH2 Amidated Asp.
谷氨酰胺Glutamine Gln Q -CH2CH2CONH2 AmidatedGlu.
